The mark

THE MARK

A quaver, fattened until it stops being notation and starts being a cartoon, then printed twice slightly out of register the way cheap comics were. The second impression is what gives it the shove.

  • Clear space is one stem width on every side.
  • The second impression always falls down and right, never the other way.
  • Below 20px drop the second impression; the note alone still reads.
  • Never outline it. The mark is solid shapes, so overlaps merge.

Android launcher

  • Violet background layer, note on the foreground.
  • The dashed ring is the 66dp safe zone. The note is centred on its own measured bounds, not its box, so the flag does not shove it off-centre inside a circle.
  • Themed icons get the mono layer.
The kit

THE KIT

Three flats on warm paper, a 3px ink stroke on everything, and a hard offset shadow instead of a blur. Objects move onto their own shadow when pressed.
