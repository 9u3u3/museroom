Museroom Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 September 2026

Museroom shows what you are listening to, shares it with friends you add, and counts your listening minutes for a leaderboard. This page describes exactly what it reads, what it keeps, and what other people can see.

What the app reads on your phone

Android publishes a media session for whichever app is playing audio. Museroom reads that session to learn the track title, artist, album, artwork, duration and playback position.

Reading it requires notification access. Museroom does not read the contents of your notifications. It filters to music players before anything is inspected, and you choose which players count from within the app.

What is stored on your phone

  • Playback events: play, pause, seek, track change, and a periodic heartbeat.
  • Reconstructed listening sessions and the minutes credited to each.
  • Your sign-in tokens, if you sign in.

If you never sign in, none of this leaves your device.

What is sent to the server

Once you sign in, the app uploads your playback events, your listening sessions and what you are currently playing. This is what makes friends and the leaderboard work. Data is stored in Supabase, hosted on Amazon Web Services.

Signing in with Google gives Museroom your email address and basic profile information. It does not give access to your Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google service.

Who can see your listening

  • What you are playing right now is visible according to your visibility setting: everyone, friends only, or nobody. The default is friends only.
  • Your handle and total minutes appear on the public leaderboard if you are opted in. You can opt out and keep using everything else.
  • Your detailed history and event log are visible only to you. This is enforced by database policy, not only by the app.

Deleting your data

You can clear your local history from within the app. To delete your account and everything associated with it on the server, email the address below and it will be removed. Deleting the app from your phone removes everything stored locally.

Children

Museroom is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
