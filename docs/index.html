Museroom

See what your friends are listening to, in real time.

Museroom reads whatever is playing on your Android phone, whether that is Spotify, YouTube Music or another player, and shows it to the friends you add. It counts your listening minutes and ranks them on a leaderboard.

How it works

  • Android keeps a media session for the app currently playing audio. Museroom reads the track and its position from there, so it works with players that have no public interface of their own.
  • Your listening is counted on the phone and uploaded once you sign in.
  • Nothing is shared until you sign in, and what friends can see is yours to set.

Installing

Museroom is distributed as an APK rather than through the Play Store. Download the latest release, allow installation from unknown sources when Android asks, and grant notification access when the app requests it. That permission is what makes detection possible.

Privacy

The app reads music players only, and filters everything else out before it is inspected. The full privacy policy sets out what is stored and who can see it.
