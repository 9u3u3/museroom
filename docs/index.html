What it counts

Spotify and YouTube Music, including the ReVanced and RVX builds. Nothing else on your device is ever read — browsers are deliberately unsupported, since a browser plays whatever the web plays.

How it works

  • Reads the track straight from Android's own media session — works even with players that have no public interface of their own.
  • A listening room plays through YouTube Music inside the app, matched by name and kept in step. No audio ever crosses phones.
  • Ad slots are stripped from the player data before it loads — the same trick the YouTube Music desktop clients use.
  • Nearby runs on Bluetooth Low Energy with a rotating token, never location — which is why Android 12+ doesn't even ask for it.
  • Nothing leaves your phone until you sign in, and visibility is always yours to set.

Installing it

Not on the Play Store, so Android puts two warnings in the way. Neither means anything's wrong — both are explained below, and together they take about a minute.

  1. Download & open the file. Allow installs from your browser when it asks.
  2. Get past Play Protect — a couple of taps, explained below.
  3. Grant notification access on first launch. If Android blocks it, see below.
Warning 1

"Unsafe app blocked" — Play Protect

Google flags any app not from the Play Store as unrecognised — not unsafe, just unseen. Tap More details → Install anyway (some phones say Install without scanning).

This can't be switched off from our side — only Play Store distribution removes it. Check the app yourself: it's open on GitHub.

Warning 2

"Restricted setting" — Android 13+

Notification access is how Museroom reads the current track, nothing more. Android hides that toggle behind a Restricted setting block for any app installed outside the Play Store.

Fix: Settings → Apps → Museroom → find Allow restricted settings:

  • Behind the ⋮ menu, on most phones
  • Under Advanced or More, on some
  • Near the bottom of the page, on others

Missing entirely? Reinstall and check within a few minutes — it only shows up right after installing. Do it once and it sticks, as long as you update instead of uninstalling.

adb shell appops set com.museroom.app ACCESS_RESTRICTED_SETTINGS allow
Coming from an older version? Uninstall first — 3.8 changed the signing key, once. Every version after this updates in place, so don't uninstall again: notification access resets otherwise.
